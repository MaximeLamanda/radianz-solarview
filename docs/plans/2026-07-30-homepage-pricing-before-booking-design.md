# Design: Homepage Pricing Blocks before Booking

**Date:** 2026-07-30  
**Status:** Approved

## Goal

Show two pricing offers on the homepage immediately before `LetsTalkSection` (booking): free AI Audit + Web & AI Platform from 5 000 €, with feature checklists (Impulse Lab–inspired).

## Decisions

- Reuse existing `PricingSection` (no `/services` page).
- Home only.
- Offer 1: Audit IA — price **Gratuit** / **Free**.
- Offer 2 (highlighted): Plateforme web & IA — **À partir de 5 000 €** / **From 5,000 €**.
- CTAs → `/contact`.

## Out of scope

- Contact page pricing
- Visual redesign beyond existing component
- Paid audit pricing
